Return to the Source

Transformative African Cinema Leadership

A groundbreaking Pan-African film lab that bridges history, healing, and future narratives. We redefine film education as both artistic expression and therapeutic intervention, creating a transformative approach to confronting colonial legacies and reclaiming African stories.

Our Mission & Vision

We combine cinematic expertise, industry development, and trauma-informed care to create a holistic approach that serves individual artistic development while contributing to broader community healing and cultural reclamation.


Our methodology treats history as material, landscape as archive, and healing as protocol - creating new pathways for authentic African storytelling.

Four-Pillar Leadership Team

Gender-balanced expertise spanning decolonial theory, strategic production, visionary filmmaking, and trauma-informed healing

Dr. Nashilongweshipwe Mushaandja

Theoretical Architect & Cultural Methodologist

Distinguished scholar-artist whose groundbreaking theoretical frameworks transform indigenous knowledge systems into sophisticated decolonial methodologies. Creator of the "Oudano" praxis that bridges traditional wisdom with contemporary creative practice.

Key Contributions
  • Lecturer, Drama & Theatre Arts, University of Namibia
  • Developer of "Oudano" decolonial methodology
  • Zürcher Kantonalbank Award Winner 2021
  • RTS 2024: "Landscape as Archive" masterclass

Fibby Kioria

Strategic Producer & Network Architect

Visionary producer with eight years leading Maisha Film Lab, pioneering African film distribution through innovative networks, and building Pan-African cultural infrastructure. Creates pathways for authentic African storytelling to reach global audiences.

Key Contributions
  • Founder & CEO, Mucii Pictures
  • Co-founder, East African Screen Collective
  • €227,000 DW Akademie Fund Manager
  • African Film Festivals Platform architect

Perivi Katjavivi

Visionary Filmmaker & Historical Narrative Specialist

Pioneering filmmaker whose innovative exploration of African identity positions him at the forefront of African narrative sovereignty. Director of Namibia's first-ever Academy Award submission, establishing new pathways for African cinema globally.

Key Contributions
  • Director, "Under the Hanging Tree" (Namibia's first Oscar submission)
  • Founder, Old Location Films
  • Two AMAA Awards Winner 2023
  • PhD candidate exploring post-colonial cinema

Marcella Katjijova

Healing Architect & Trauma-Informed Guide

Ovaherero genocide descendant and registered psychological counselor specializing in intergenerational trauma. Transforms film education into therapeutic intervention, ensuring participants process historical material safely while reclaiming cultural narratives.

Key Contributions
  • Head of Trauma & Recovery, Ovaherero Genocide Foundation
  • Global Cultural Assembly Reference Group Member
  • Registered Psychological Counselor
  • Published researcher in trauma therapy
