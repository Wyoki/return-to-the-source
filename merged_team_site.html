Perivi Katjavivi

Founder & Creative Director

Background

Nationality: Namibian

Focus: Identity, Memory, History

Specialization: Pan-African Storytelling

Role at Return to Source

As founder, Perivi provides the creative vision and philosophical foundation for the film lab's approach to decolonial filmmaking practices.

Areas of Expertise

• Academic research in cinema
• Cultural identity exploration
• Historical narrative development
• Cross-cultural collaboration

Vision & Philosophy

Perivi Katjavivi brings a unique academic and artistic perspective to Return to the Source, combining scholarly research with practical filmmaking experience. His work explores the intersection of personal and collective memory, examining how cinema can serve as a tool for cultural healing and identity formation in post-colonial African contexts.

Academic Foundation

His research in cinema and cultural studies provides the theoretical framework that distinguishes Return to the Source from traditional film training programs. This academic grounding ensures that practical filmmaking skills are taught within a broader context of cultural and historical understanding.

Decolonial Filmmaking Practices

Perivi's approach to filmmaking challenges conventional Western narrative structures, instead drawing from African storytelling traditions and epistemologies. This methodology helps participants develop authentic voices that honor their cultural heritage while creating contemporary cinema.

Pan-African Collaboration

His vision extends beyond individual filmmaker development to creating networks of collaboration across the African continent. Through Return to the Source, he fosters connections that enable knowledge sharing and collective growth within African cinema communities.
